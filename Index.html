Blackjack statistics — quick reference

Per-hand math (basic strategy, 0.5% edge)
• Win (incl. BJ): ~43.9%
• Loss: ~47.7%
• Push: ~8.4%
• Blackjack 3:2: ~4.75%
• EV per $1: -$0.005
• SD per $1: $1.14

N-hand formulas
EV_N = N × bet × (-edge)
SD_N = bet × SD_hand × √N
• 95% range ≈ EV ± 1.96×SD
• Risk of Ruin ≈ exp(-2×edge×Bankroll / variance) for infinite horizon
Interpretation guide
• Histogram width = variance. Wider = swingier. Std dev grows √time, EV grows linearly negative.
• Right tail = luck. ~16% finish > Mean+1SD. Doubling bankroll in 4h at $25/$1000 is ~6–9%.
• Ruin risk scales with units. 40 units (2.5% bet): 4h ruin ~2–5%, 8h ~5–12%. 20 units: ~3× higher.
• Optimal stop? Mathematically: immediately. EV_N is strictly decreasing. Practically: set a 30–50% stop-loss and a 40–60% take-profit — you cap left tail, and accept you’ll usually walk away small-down.
• Martingale fails: it converts many small wins into rare total ruin. SD explodes.
